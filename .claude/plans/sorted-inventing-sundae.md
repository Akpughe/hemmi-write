# AI Detection Reduction Implementation Plan

## Problem Statement
Content generated by Hemmi-Write is being flagged by AI detectors. This plan implements humanization techniques to reduce detection rates.

## User Decisions
- **Humanization Mode**: Always On (applied to all content automatically)
- **Phrase Enforcement**: Strict (all banned phrases explicitly listed in every prompt)

---

## How AI Detectors Work (Research Summary)

| Metric | What It Measures | AI Pattern | Human Pattern |
|--------|-----------------|------------|---------------|
| **Perplexity** | Word choice predictability | Low (predictable) | High (varied/creative) |
| **Burstiness** | Sentence length variation | Uniform | Varied rhythm |

### Detection Triggers to Address:
- Overused phrases: "In today's world", "delve", "landscape", "tapestry", "Furthermore", "Moreover"
- Uniform sentence lengths (monotonous rhythm)
- Passive voice overuse
- Generic vague language vs specific concrete details
- **Em-dash (—) overuse** - AI models use em-dashes where humans use commas/parentheses
- Overly smooth/perfect grammar

### Em-Dash Rule (Critical)
AI models significantly overuse em-dashes. The rule:
- **NEVER use em-dashes (—)** in generated content
- Replace with **commas** in most cases
- Use **parentheses** for asides
- Use **colons** for explanations
- Example: "The study—which was groundbreaking—showed..." → "The study, which was groundbreaking, showed..."

## NEW: Additional Research Findings (2025-2026)

### NEW: High-Frequency AI Phrases (Quantified)
According to GPTZero research:
- **"plays a significant role in shaping"** - 207x more frequent in AI than human writing
- **"has sparked"**, **"has ignited"**, **"has been acknowledged"** - agentless passive constructions AI overuses
- **Elaborate subject substitution** - AI changes "ChatGPT" to "the rising influence of ChatGPT within academia"

### NEW: ChatGPT Writing Fingerprint (Academic Research)
From PMC studies on ChatGPT detection:
- **Paragraph structure**: 3-4 sentences, 16-19 words each (too consistent)
- **High lexical density, low lexical diversity**: Many content words but repetitive vocabulary
- **Neutral sentiment**: AI expresses fewer negative emotions than humans
- **Declarative mood dominance**: Lacks questions and varied sentence types
- **Overly simplified conclusions**: Lacks complexity and nuance expected in academic writing

### NEW: What Human Writers Do That AI Doesn't
1. **Equivocal language**: Scientists frequently use "but", "however", "although" - shows nuance
2. **Contractions**: "I'm" vs "I am", "doesn't" vs "does not"
3. **Idioms and colloquialisms**: Natural expressions AI avoids
4. **Natural connectors**: "that said", "on the flip side", "you know"
5. **Minor imperfections**: Sentence fragments, starting with "And" or "But"
6. **Emotional language**: Including negative sentiments, frustration, surprise
7. **Personal anecdotes**: Real stories and lived experiences

### NEW: Why Simple Paraphrasing Fails
- AI detectors in 2025-2026 analyze deeper linguistic patterns, not just surface text
- GPTZero claims 99% accuracy
- Single-click "spin" features don't change rhythm or clause order
- Need **layered approach**: syntax reshuffling + phrase expansion + idiom injection

### NEW: Successful Bypass Strategy
From detection research:
1. Mix sentence structure: complex → fragment → question
2. Alternate short/medium/long sentences
3. Add contextual details AI typically overlooks
4. Use active voice: "The team hammered out that report" not "The report was written"
5. Test iteratively until 90%+ human-like scores

### Preferred Alternatives
```
"Furthermore" → "Beyond this", "Building on this point", "This connects to"
"Moreover" → "Also worth noting", "A related point", "Similarly"
"delve" → "examine", "explore", "investigate", "look at", "analyze"
"landscape" → "field", "area", "domain", "space", "context"
"crucial" → "important", "key", "significant", "central"
```

### Writing Techniques
1. **Sentence Variation**: Mix short (5-10 words), medium (11-20), long (21-35) - avoid ChatGPT's 16-19 word average
2. **Imperfect Flow**: Don't connect every sentence with transitions. Just start. Let readers connect.
3. **Specificity**: "23% reduction" not "significant decrease"
4. **Natural Imperfection**: Start with "And"/"But", use fragments, contractions ("doesn't" not "does not")
5. **Active Voice**: "The team hammered out the report" not "The report was written by the team"
6. **NO Em-Dashes**: Never use em-dashes (—). Always use commas, parentheses, or colons instead
7. **NEW: Equivocal Language**: Use "but", "however", "although" to show nuance (scientists do this)
8. **NEW: Natural Connectors**: "that said", "on the flip side", "here's the thing"
9. **NEW: Emotional Variation**: Include occasional frustration, surprise, or strong opinion
10. **NEW: Varied Paragraph Length**: Don't default to 3-4 sentences per paragraph
11. **NEW: Questions**: Mix in rhetorical questions to break declarative monotony

### Academic Level Personas
- **Undergraduate**: Clear, engaged student voice. Allows contractions. Shows learning journey.
- **Graduate**: Confident academic voice. Critical engagement. Synthesizes sources.
- **Postgraduate**: Authoritative scholarly voice. Original contributions. Deep theoretical engagement.
- **Professional**: Direct, action-oriented. Practical recommendations. Business idioms allowed.
